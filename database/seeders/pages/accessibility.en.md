## Accessibility statement

:name aims to make this online shop accessible in line with the German Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG). The benchmark is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA (EN 301 549).

### What the shop offers

You can browse books, add them to the cart, order and pay, view your orders and invoices in the customer account, and withdraw from a contract. All functions work with a keyboard and a screen reader. Under “Display” in the header you can adjust colour scheme, text size and link styling. The shop is set in black on white (or white on black) throughout and has no animations.

### Conformance status

The shop is tested automatically (axe, Lighthouse) and manually with the keyboard. We are not aware of any barriers at present. The payment pages of external payment providers are outside our control.

### Report a barrier

If you encounter a barrier, write to us: :email. We usually reply within two weeks.

### Market surveillance

The competent authority is the Marktüberwachungsstelle der Länder für die Barrierefreiheit von Produkten und Dienstleistungen (MLBF), based in Magdeburg, Germany.
