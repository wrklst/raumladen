## Withdrawal information

The German version is the binding one; this translation is for your convenience.

### Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day on which you, or a third party named by you who is not the carrier, took possession of the goods. If you ordered several goods in one order that are delivered separately, the period starts on the day on which you, or a third party named by you who is not the carrier, took possession of the last of the goods.

To exercise your right of withdrawal you must inform us (:name, :address1, :zip :city, Germany, email :email) of your decision to withdraw from this contract by an unequivocal statement (for example a letter sent by post or an email). You may use the model withdrawal form below, but it is not obligatory. You can also declare your withdrawal electronically with the function “Withdraw from contract” at the foot of every page of this shop. If you use it, we send you a confirmation of receipt of your withdrawal by email without delay.

To meet the withdrawal deadline it is sufficient to send your notice before the withdrawal period has expired.

### Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we reimburse all payments received from you, including the costs of delivery (except the additional costs resulting from your choice of a type of delivery other than the least expensive standard delivery offered by us), without undue delay and no later than fourteen days from the day on which we received your notice of withdrawal. We use the same means of payment that you used for the original transaction, unless expressly agreed otherwise with you; you will not be charged any fees for this reimbursement.

We may withhold reimbursement until we have received the goods back or until you have supplied evidence of having sent them back, whichever is earlier.

You must send back or hand over the goods to us (:name, :address1, :zip :city, Germany) without undue delay and no later than fourteen days from the day on which you inform us of your withdrawal. The deadline is met if you send the goods before the fourteen days have expired.

You bear the direct cost of returning the goods.

You are only liable for any diminished value of the goods resulting from handling other than what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.
