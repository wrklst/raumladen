With your first order we create a customer account for your email address, where you can view orders and invoices. To sign in we send a one-time code to your email address; we do not store passwords for customers. You can ask us to delete the account at any time; statutory retention duties for invoices remain.

### 8. VAT ID check

If you order as a business and give a VAT identification number, we check it with the European Commission's VAT Information Exchange System (VIES). Only the number is transmitted. Tax law obliges us to do so (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

### 9. Address suggestions

While you type a street at checkout we suggest matching addresses. The search runs through our own server, which passes the typed text and the selected country to the Photon service (komoot GmbH, Potsdam; OpenStreetMap data) and caches the answer. Your IP address and your name are not transmitted.

### 10. Cookies and local storage

We only set technically necessary cookies (§ 25(2) TDDDG): a session cookie for cart, language and sign-in, a cookie that protects forms and, if you stay signed in, a sign-in cookie. If you choose a colour scheme, a text size or underlined links under “Display”, we store that choice in a cookie and in your browser's local storage; nothing is stored there unless you choose. We use no third-party advertising or tracking services; for our own counting of visits see section 11. Fonts and scripts are loaded from our own server.

### 11. Audience measurement without cookies

We count the visits to this shop ourselves, without cookies and without storing anything in your browser. Recorded are the page opened, the language, the time spent, the device class (phone, tablet, computer), the referring website and campaign tags from the address. Your IP address and your browser's identification are not stored: from both, a key is computed with a random value that changes every day; the key is valid for one day and cannot be derived afterwards. There is no recognition across days, no profile and no disclosure to third parties. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in shaping the shop to its visitors' needs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is deleted after 13 months. If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, you are not counted; this is how you can object at any time.

### 12. Withdrawal through the online form

If you use the function “Withdraw from contract”, we process what you enter there to handle your withdrawal and confirm its receipt (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).
